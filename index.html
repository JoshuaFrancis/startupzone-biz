Zone programmes

Three tracks for every stage of the startup journey.

01

Pre-seed cohort

A structured 12-week programme for founders with an idea and a team but no revenue. Covers customer discovery, MVP scoping, go-to-market framing, and pitch preparation, culminating in a demo day for Zone-affiliated investors.

02

Growth residency

A six-month deep residency for post-revenue startups actively raising a seed or Series A. Zone residents receive a dedicated advisor, weekly founder office hours, investor introductions, and priority access to corporate partnership programmes.

03

Flex membership

Monthly access to Zone co-working, event programming, and the mentor network without a cohort commitment. Ideal for solo founders, freelancers building toward a product, or remote teams that need a Canadian base of operations.

Who belongs here

For founders who are serious about building something that lasts.

First-time founders

Entrepreneurs launching their first venture who want structure, accountability, and access to people who have done it before. The Zone's cohort format provides the peer group that most first-time founders are missing and need most at the early stage.

Repeat founders

Operators who have built before and want a Canadian base with warm introductions to the local investor community, government innovation programmes, and a co-working environment where the people around them are building at the same pace.

International founders

Global entrepreneurs expanding into Canada or relocating through immigration pathways including the Start-Up Visa. The Zone provides the Canadian operational footprint, designated organization connections, and advisor support the application process requires.
